# Terms of Service — operator template

This is a product template and must be reviewed for the operator's jurisdiction before publication.

The customer controls its procurement decisions and remains responsible for approvals, supplier due
diligence, sanctions checks, taxes, purchases, and submitted data. ProcureX supplies workflow,
evidence, and audit tooling; it does not execute payments or autonomously authorize purchases.

An executed order form should define the subscribed plan, users, limits, price, billing period,
support target, hosting profile, data region, retention, termination, liability, governing law, and
security commitments. Prohibit unlawful use, credential sharing, malicious uploads, unauthorized
access, and attempts to bypass tenant controls.
